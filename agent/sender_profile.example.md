## Who We Are
Quantra Energy is an AI-driven forecasting company serving utilities,
retailers, and grid operators. We help energy-sector teams make better
decisions by turning noisy operational and market data into accurate,
actionable forecasts.

## What We Do
We build machine-learning models for short-term renewable generation and
demand forecasting, then plug them directly into a customer's existing
dispatch, trading, or grid-operations workflows — no rip-and-replace.

## Key Differentiators
- Deployed and proven with prominent Indian and US-based energy utilities
  navigating volatile renewable assets.
- Forecasts are built for operational use (dispatch, trading desks), not just
  reporting dashboards.
- Fast integration — designed to slot into a customer's stack in days, not
  months.

## Proof Points / Case Studies
- Reduced forecast error by 20-35% for utility and trading-desk customers,
  directly improving dispatch accuracy and P&L.

## Technology & Overlap Keywords
machine learning, renewable forecasting, solar nowcasting, grid optimization,
dispatch algorithms, carbon-aware dispatch, demand forecasting, real-time
operational data, high-resolution meteorological data.

## Target Customers & Why They Buy
Utilities, retailers, and grid operators who need to manage volatile
renewable assets and want forecasting accuracy that plugs into live
operational decisions — not just historical analysis.
